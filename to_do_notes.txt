Demurrage

The ship is delayed beyond the agreed loading/unloading time ("laytime").

The cargo owner (charterer) pays the ship owner.

Example:

Allowed laytime: 3 days
Actual loading: 5 days

2 days late

Demurrage:
$15,000/day

Ship owner receives:
$30,000
🚢 Dispatch

The opposite.

Loading/unloading finishes faster than allowed.

The ship owner pays the charterer a reward.

Example:

Allowed laytime: 3 days
Actual loading: 2 days

1 day saved

Dispatch:
$7,500/day

Ship owner pays:
$7,500

Dispatch is often about 50% of the demurrage rate, though it varies.
