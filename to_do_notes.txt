My recommendation

I don't think you've found a new Production filter yet.

I do think you've found the next research direction.

If I were prioritising the next experiments, I would do them in this order:

Optimise Speed Trend thresholds ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Optimise Barrier Improvement thresholds ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆
Optimise Distance Change thresholds ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆
Investigate three-way interactions (for example, improving speed + similar distance + barrier improvement) only after the individual thresholds are well understood.

This is the first time in the Finishing Strength work that I think the results have identified a genuinely promising avenue for further model improvement, but they still need larger-sample optimisation before they would justify changes to your production betting strategy.
